FAQ

Questions we get on every call.

Can I keep my existing phone number?

Yes. You keep your number. We set up a separate line that your business number forwards missed calls to, so customers don't notice a change. They just get a text back instead of silence.

What if I already have voicemail?

Voicemail is passive. Customers leave a message and hope you call back. Our system is active: it texts them within 30 seconds and routes them to book, get an estimate, or ask a question. Most people don't leave voicemails anymore. They hang up and call the next business.

Does AI actually talk to my customers?

On Growth and Elite, yes. When a customer texts back a question, AI reads it and responds right away, with no wait and no owner follow-up needed. On Starter, questions are forwarded to you directly. Either way, no lead falls through the cracks.

How long does setup take?

3–5 business days from your onboarding call to going live.

What happens if I cancel?

Missed-call plans are month-to-month, so you can cancel anytime with no penalty. We turn off the system, and your number goes back to ringing normally. No contracts, no lock-in.

How does pricing work?

Missed-call plans are a flat monthly fee, billed monthly or prepaid for 6 or 12 months at a lower rate. There's a one-time $500 setup fee, waived for the first 10 clients. Websites, CRM, portals, and workflow automation are quoted after a short call.
