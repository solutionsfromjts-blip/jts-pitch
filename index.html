How a missed call gets rescued

One missed call, start to finish.

Scroll to follow a single missed call through the system. This is an example, not a real customer.

  1. 1The call rings and goes unanswered

    You're on a job and can't pick up. The customer is still calling.

  2. 2An auto-text goes out in 30 seconds

    Before the customer calls someone else, they get a text back with options to book, ask a question, or request a callback.

  3. 3The customer replies

    They pick an option or ask a question, and the conversation keeps going.

  4. 4The job gets booked

    The time they choose lands on your calendar with no back-and-forth.

  5. 5You get the details

    Name, need, and time arrive on your phone, so you know what's booked.

FAQ

Questions we get on every call.

Do I need to be tech-savvy?

Not at all. We configure everything, so you don't touch a single tool or setting. For missed-call automation, the most technical thing you do is forward your business number, which takes about 60 seconds, and we walk you through it.

What are the most common tools you use?

We stick with proven, widely used platforms we know well, so your business isn't a testing ground. The ones we use most: Twilio for texting and phone numbers, Zapier to connect your apps and automate steps, Calendly and Google Calendar for booking, Airtable for records, logs, and CRM data, and Softr for client portals and web apps. We pick the right mix for your business after a short call.

How does pricing work?

Missed-call automation plans are a flat monthly fee, plus a one-time setup fee that's waived for the first 10 clients. Websites, CRM, portals, and custom work are quoted after a short call, based on what you need.

What happens if I cancel?

Missed-call plans are month-to-month, so you can cancel anytime with no penalty. We'll turn off the system and your number goes back to ringing normally. No contracts, no lock-in, no hard feelings.

Can you build a customer portal or web app?

Yes. We build client portals, dashboards, and web apps around your workflow, so customers and your team can log in, track work, and get answers without the back-and-forth. We build on platforms like Airtable and Softr. Every project is scoped to your needs, so we quote after a short call.
